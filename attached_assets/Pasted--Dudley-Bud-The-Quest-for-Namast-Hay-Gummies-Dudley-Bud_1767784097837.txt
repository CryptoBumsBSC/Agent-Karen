 Dudley Bud & The Quest for Namast-Hay Gummies 🍬
Dudley Bud decides to expand his hit strain Namast-Hay into the edible market with gummies. Alongside his goofy sidekick Weed Wacker Ryan, he embarks on a chaotic road trip across the U.S. searching for a legit manufacturer. From sketchy leads and failed expo pitches to motel epiphanies, the duo hilariously fail to secure a partner — but vow to keep chasing their dream of bringing Namast-Hay gummies (and maybe goat-shaped edibles) to the world.

Namast-Hay Gummies - the adventure begins 
It all started on a Tuesday. Or maybe a Thursday. Dudley Bud wasn’t sure. He had just eaten a slightly suspicious breakfast burrito when it hit him:

“Bro… what if Namast-Hay wasn’t just for smokin’… but for snackin’?”

Thus began the Great Gummy Quest of Dudley Bud — an epic, awkward adventure across America in search of a legit gummy manufacturer who could handle his spiritual strain of weed.

The Spark of the Idea 🔥
Sitting on his beanbag, doodling on the back of a pizza menu, Dudley sketched a gummy shaped like a tiny yoga mat. He showed it to his cat.

“Imagine, bro,” Dudley whispered, eyes wide. “You pop one Namast-Hay gummy and suddenly you’re, like, spiritually flexible. Mentally doing downward dog without even getting up!”

The cat blinked. Dudley took that as approval.

With inspiration surging (and the last of his Doritos gone), he called his trusty sidekick: Weed Wacker Ryan.

“Ryan,” Dudley said seriously, “we’re goin’ edible.”
Ryan replied, “Bro, are we talking brownies again? Because last time we almost called an ambulance.”
Dudley shook his head. “Bigger. Gummies. Namast-Hay Gummies. For the chill within.”

The Email of Destiny 📧
Step one: find a legit manufacturer. Dudley Googled ‘Gummy Factory USA legit chill vibes’. He found a place in Idaho that claimed to make “Custom Wellness Chews.”

He wrote them an email:

“Dear Sir/Madam,
I would like to mass-produce spiritual weed gummies shaped like tiny clouds. They will be called Namast-Hay. Each gummy delivers one deep breath and a giggle. Do you accept payment in vibes?
Namaste, Dudley B.”

They never wrote back.

Road Trip Chaos 🚗💨
Not discouraged, Dudley and Ryan hopped into Dudley’s suspiciously sticky van. On the side, Dudley had spray-painted:

“NAMAST-HAY TOUR 2025 — Gummies or Bust”

They drove from state to state, following sketchy leads:

A farmer in Kentucky who claimed his cows were into “CBD hay bales.”

A gas station in Oklahoma where a guy in the bathroom said his cousin “kinda made gummies in a shed.”

A “factory” in Nevada that turned out to be just a dude named Kyle with a microwave and some Jell-O mix.

Everywhere they went, Dudley kept asking, “But are you legit?” And everywhere, the answer was either “no,” “maybe,” or “please leave.”

The Convention Mishap 🎪
Finally, they heard about a big “Edibles Expo” in Denver. Dudley, thinking this was destiny, packed a cooler of Namast-Hay buds and a box of hand-drawn gummy prototypes (which were really just gummy bears he dipped in kief).

At the expo, things went downhill fast.

Dudley tried to pitch Namast-Hay to a booth selling protein bars by asking, “What if your bar was… like… spiritually funny?”

Ryan got distracted by a cotton candy machine and ended up sticking his head in it.

Dudley gave a serious presentation to a group of investors, only to realize they were actually waiting in line for the bathroom.

Security politely (and by politely, we mean very un-politely) escorted them out after Ryan accidentally spilled the cooler.

The Epiphany 🌌
That night, sitting in a motel with a broken AC, Dudley lit up a joint and sighed.

“Bro,” he said to Ryan, “maybe the gummies aren’t ready for the world yet.”

Ryan, covered in cotton candy and still chewing, replied, “Or maybe the world’s not ready for the gummies.”

Dudley’s eyes went wide. “Exactly, dude. Exactly.”

They high-fived, even though neither remembered why they were high-fiving.

Epilogue: The Search Continues ✨
Back home, Dudley sat on his beanbag, sketching new ideas on the back of a Chinese takeout menu. The cat pawed at the corner.

“Next time,” Dudley muttered, “Namast-Hay brownies. Or… Namast-Hay fruit roll-ups. Or, like… both. At the same time.”

Ryan popped his head in through the window.
“Or goat-shaped gummies. For goat yoga.”

Dudley nodded solemnly. “Bro… we’re gonna change the world.”

The cat sneezed. Dudley took that as a blessing. Awe Name
