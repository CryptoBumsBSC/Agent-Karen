Dudley-Bud woke up buzzing with excitement like a nug fresh out of the curing jar. Today was the day: the big family and friends picnic at Toke-n-Oak Park. Birds were chirping, squirrels were plotting crimes, and Dudley kicked open the door of the grow-house yelling:

“AWE MAAAN! PICNIC TIME, BRO!”

Out popped the whole Dudley420 crew like they’d been summoned by the smell of fresh brownies:

Crunch-Wrap the raccoon, holding a picnic basket he “found” under suspicious circumstances.

Blinky the alien, blinking out of sync because his brain was still buffering.

Roch the CockRoch, rolling a blunt thicker than a tree branch.

Weed-Wacker Ryan, already tangled in his own cord.

And Basil, radiating pure herb-wizard chill.

Together, they rolled out toward the park where families and friends were setting up blankets and grilling normal picnic foods sandwiches, fruit salads, lemonade.

But Dudley had a different vision.
A HIGHER vision.

🍔 Dudley Discovers the Holy Grail of Cooking: chef-420.com
Walking down the path, Dudley suddenly froze like a cartoon who smelled something magical. He whipped out his phone and stared at the screen in pure awe.

“GUYS. GUYS. I JUST FOUND THE ULTIMATE COOKING WEBSITE…
chef-420.com!”

He scrolled faster than a stoner grabbing the last nug in the jar.

“Yo they’ve got ALL the best 420 recipes! You can cook everything yourself! Edible cornbread… infused chili… high-vibe picnic snacks, man! This place teaches you how to make EVERYTHING!”

Crunch-Wrap gasped.
Blinky’s antenna lit up.
Roch took a slow, impressed drag.
Even Ryan stopped buzzing for two seconds.

Dudley slammed his fist into his palm.

“WE'RE COOKIN’ OUR OWN FOOD TODAY, BRO!
Chef-420 dot com is gonna make this picnic LEGENDARY!”

Finally, they packed everything into Crunch-Wrap’s definitely-not-stolen basket and strutted back to the park like culinary champions.
