Dudley’s Grow-op Saga – Part 3: The Great Harvest Heist
Previously on Dudley’s Grow-op Saga: In Part 1, Dudley got super inspired by a YouTube video called “Ganja Gardens of the Galaxy” and decided to turn his linen closet into a grow room. He bought a suspicious hydro kit from a guy named Vape Carl and named his plants things like "Lil Buddy" and "Captain Greenbeard." In Part 2, the plants somehow thrived despite Dudley’s questionable methods (including talking to them exclusively in Bob Marley lyrics and feeding them leftover kombucha). A raccoon began hanging around the back porch, possibly high, possibly plotting something. And now…

(A continuation of Dudley's chaotic journey into the world of amateur hydroponics, dubious horticultural wisdom, and one deeply suspicious raccoon.)
It was a dewy Tuesday morning when Dudley stumbled out of bed, tripped over his bong (“Sir Bubblesworth”), and declared:

“Today’s the day. Harvest time, baby.”

He had no idea how he knew it was harvest time. It wasn’t based on trichome color or pistil maturity. No. It was based on vibe.
The plants looked like they were peaking. Also, one of them had started humming? Unconfirmed.

So Dudley rolled up his sleeves (well, he cut the sleeves off), grabbed his dullest pair of kitchen scissors, and opened the closet.

#1: The Plants Were...Gone?

Well, not gone. But half of “Captain Greenbeard” had been mauled. The buds were missing, and there were suspicious muddy paw prints leading straight out the window.

Dudley gasped. “Rick the raccoon, you thieving little fluff pirate.”

This led to 30 minutes of Dudley stumbling around the yard in Crocs, shaking a bag of Doritos like a maraca, whisper-shouting:

“Come out, Rick! I just wanna talk!”

No sign of Rick. Just a half-buried garden gnome that hadn’t been there before and smelled faintly of skunk. Coincidence?

#2: Dudley Didn't Know How to Trim

Once inside, Dudley turned his attention to the salvageable buds. But his idea of trimming was... interpretive. He used manicure scissors, a salad spinner, and at one point, a toothbrush.

He watched a tutorial but got distracted by an ad for vegan nachos and never made it past “Step One: Be Patient.”

So he trimmed everything into what can only be described as a pile of mossy tumbleweeds, then proudly packed it into mason jars… while still damp.

He labeled the jars things like:

“Dank Sinatra”

“Funky Grandpa”

“Spicy Sadness?”

#3: The Mold Strikes Back

Two days later, he opened a jar and was hit with the unmistakable scent of death and regret. White fuzz covered his precious buds like a haunted chia pet.

Dudley stared at the jar, exhaled slowly, and whispered,

“This is why I can’t have nice things.”

He salvaged what he could, probably to the dismay of his lungs, and decided to bury the moldy ones in the backyard next to his failed sourdough starter (“Yeasty Boy”).

Final Crisis: Rick Returns

That night, Dudley was woken by a crash. He ran to the kitchen and caught Rick the raccoon, bold as ever, standing in the fridge, holding a jar of “Spicy Sadness?” and eating peanut butter with a spoon.

They locked eyes.

Dudley, in awe:

“Bro… you have thumbs?”

Rick dropped the spoon, flipped him off (?), and scurried into the night—jar in tow.

 
Dudley decided maybe he wasn’t ready for the grow life. Not yet. But he was ready for a new hobby: Awe Man!

 

Mushrooms.
