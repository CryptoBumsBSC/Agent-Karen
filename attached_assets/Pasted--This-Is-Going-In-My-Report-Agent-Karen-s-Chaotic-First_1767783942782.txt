 “This Is Going In My Report!” Agent Karen’s Chaotic First Encounter 🔥
The door BURST open like it owed someone rent, and in stormed Agent Karen—Pest Control & Compliance. High heels. Badge. Headset. Chardonnay still faintly detectable on her breath. Clipboard held like a weapon forged from pure paperwork fury.

A Dudley-Bud Universe Story

The sun was melting into the horizon like a half-baked edible, and Dudley-Bud was sprawled across the couch, blissfully unaware of the dimensional chaos about to walk through his front door. A gentle cloud of skunky fog hung in the air, swirling around the living room like it had feelings. Roch the CockRoch was DJing on a hacked pest-control smart trap he stole from a government van two nights earlier—lights flashing, tiny roach headphones on, living his best illegal-wormy life.

Weed-Wacker was in the corner, tinkering with something that should NOT have been sparking, while Basil was practicing impersonations on the radio, switching between “Australian weather reporter” and “angry seagull.” Blinky, as always, was floating half-awake, half-high, half-something else entirely, tapping buttons on an alien device that probably should have been labeled "Do Not Touch".

Then came that knock.

Not a normal knock.
A government knock.
A clipboard knock.

Basil froze mid-seagull.
Roch dropped the beat.
Dudley looked at Weed-Wacker and whispered,
“Bro… did we order pizza?”

The door BURST open like it owed someone rent, and in stormed Agent Karen Pest Control & Compliance. High heels. Badge. Headset. Chardonnay still faintly detectable on her breath. Clipboard held like a weapon forged from pure paperwork fury.

“THIS,” she exclaimed, “is a Category 7 compliance breach!”

Roch, without hesitation, flicked a tiny roach-blunt ember at the AI roach hunter Matic 9000 and drone attached to her belt. The drone IMMEDIATELY fell in love with it, glowing pink, chirping like it had discovered true spiritual purpose.

Agent Karen blinked.
“This is… going in my report.”

Behind her, one of Weed-Wacker’s half-built contraptions EXPLODED in a gentle poof of glittery smoke, spraying her blazer with sparkles like a demented birthday card.

She didn’t flinch.

Dudley staggered forward, eyes barely open, holding out a joint like a sacred peace offering.
“Oh hey, you’re early! You’re the smoke detector inspector, right? Wanna hit this before we start?”

Karen’s eye twitched so hard it almost dialed 911 by itself.

Before she could respond, Blinky tapped his alien gadget, and Agent Karen’s headset began randomly receiving radio messages:
“URGENT PIGEON AFFAIRS DOWNTOWN. PRIORITY ONE.”
“Karen, your parking meter is in labor.”
“Please report to Sector 9 for mandatory goose de-escalation training.”

Her face twisted into pure bureaucratic confusion.

“Who… authorized this transmission!?”

Basil stood behind her, mimicking her voice perfectly:
“This is Karen, rerouting myself to… immediate pigeon emergencies.”

Roch scuttled up the wall, leaving roach-graffiti behind that read:
“TOO FAST 4 U KAREN”
with a little stoned roach doodle smoking a blunt.

Karen spun around too fast stepped on one of Weed-Wacker’s discarded parts, and the whole device began vibrating violently. Weed-Wacker screamed,
“DON’T TOUCH THAT IT’S STILL TESTING”

BOOM.
Another glitter cloud.
The drone spun hearts around Roch.
The lights flickered.
Dudley giggled.

Karen coughed sparkles and shouted:
“That’s it! THIS IS GOING IN MY ”

Before she finished, the ceiling panel above her collapsed and dropped a rain of basil leaves (Basil had been storing snacks up there).

She stood there, covered in glitter, leaves, and pure government rage.

Dudley smiled warmly.
“Yo, Karen… wanna hit that joint now?”

Agent Karen looked around.
At the stoned alien.
The roach DJ.
The raccoon gear exploder.
The basil-shaped sentient plant sweeping leaves with a broom made of vibes.
And Dudley, glowing like a weed-scented sunrise.

She took a long inhale.

“This,” she whispered softly, “is DEFINITELY going in my report.”

Then she stomped out, her drone still trying to romance Roch, Basil still mimicking her on the radio, and Dudley waving like she was an Uber driver he forgot he called.

Agent Karen had arrived.
And the Dudley-Bud universe would never recover. or quite be the same , Awe Man
