Definition: Race / Ethnicity Slurs

Race or Ethnicity Slurs are words, phrases, symbols, or variations of language that are used to insult, demean, threaten, stereotype, or dehumanize an individual or group based on their race, ethnicity, skin color, national origin, ancestry, or cultural heritage.

These terms are considered harmful regardless of intent and include direct slurs, coded language, phonetic spellings, abbreviations, substitutions, or implied references commonly used to bypass moderation.

Such language contributes to discrimination, harassment, and hate, and is therefore strictly prohibited in community spaces.

Restricted Terms & Categories (Do Not List Explicit Words)

You should restrict all known and emerging variations of the following categories:

1. Anti-Black / African Descent Slurs

Any variation, spelling, abbreviation, or coded form of the N-word

Historical or modern terms used to demean people of African descent

Dehumanizing comparisons, stereotypes, or animal references tied to skin color

2. Anti-Asian Slurs

Slurs targeting East Asian, South Asian, Southeast Asian, or Pacific Islander people

Derogatory terms referencing physical traits, accents, food, or stereotypes

Pandemic-related racial blame language

3. Anti-Hispanic / Latino Slurs

Slurs targeting people of Latin American, Hispanic, or Spanish heritage

Derogatory immigration-based insults or stereotypes

Language used to portray groups as inferior, criminal, or invasive

4. Anti-Indigenous / Native Slurs

Slurs aimed at Indigenous, First Nations, Aboriginal, or Native peoples

Colonial, historical, or mascot-based dehumanizing language

Terms denying identity, humanity, or cultural legitimacy

5. Anti-Middle Eastern / Arab Slurs

Slurs targeting Arab, Persian, Kurdish, or broader Middle Eastern groups

Religious-ethnic crossover slurs (race framed as religion or vice versa)

Terrorism-based stereotyping or dehumanization

6. Anti-White / European Descent Slurs

Slurs targeting people based on white or European ancestry

Dehumanizing or collective guilt language based on skin color

Terms used to justify harassment or exclusion

7. General Dehumanizing Language

Any term comparing a race or ethnicity to animals, objects, diseases, or filth

Language implying genetic inferiority or moral deficiency

Coded hate speech or “dog whistles” meant to evade moderation

Enforcement Notes (Recommended Add-On)

This restriction applies regardless of intent, including jokes, memes, satire, or “reclaimed” usage.

Variations, misspellings, emojis, numbers, spacing, or symbols used to bypass filters are treated as violations.

Repeated or severe violations may result in warnings, content removal, suspension, or permanent bans.
