Roch the CockRoch Moves In Under Dudley Bud’s Couch
Roch the CockRoch is the newest smart-mouthed stoner in the Dudley Bud universe. After discovering a mountain of dropped snacks, ash, and chaos under Dudley Bud’s couch, Roch builds himself a tiny roach-sized home in the crumbs. While Crunchwrap the raccoon raids the food, and Weed Wacker Ryan clumsily adds to the mess, Roch chills with his mini joint, tossing sarcastic comments and fully embracing the mayhem of the Dudley Bud unit.

Cartoon scene of Roch the joint-smoking cockroach relaxing under Dudley Bud’s messy couch while Crunchwrap the raccoon and Weed Wacker Ryan make a stoner food mess in the living room
One morning, Dudley Bud woke up, rubbed his sleepy red eyes, and heard a tiny voice complaining from somewhere under the couch. It wasn’t Crunchwrap the raccoon rummaging for dropped nachos. It wasn’t Weed Wacker Ryan tripping over a bong again. No… it was someone new.

A cockroach.
A joint-smoking, smart-mouthed, attitude-loaded cockroach.
And he had already made himself at home.

Roch the CockRoch had moved in under Dudley Bud’s couch because, honestly, it was paradise down there. So many crumbs, so many chips, so many half-eaten snacks raining down like blessings from the Stoner Gods. Crunchwrap made messes daily, Ryan spilled everything he touched, and Dudley Bud himself dropped enough food to feed an insect nation. Roch didn’t just visit—he built a whole home under there. A fortress of chip crumbs, rolling paper scraps, and a tiny stash jar he “borrowed” from Ryan.

Every time Dudley Bud sat down, Roch lit his miniature joint, popped out with his little roach arms crossed, and started talking trash.

“Yo Dudley, you ever gonna vacuum? Because at this rate, I’m gonna put up a mailbox and register this couch as official housing.”

Crunchwrap and Roch instantly became frenemies—Crunchwrap tried to steal his snacks, Roch tried to lecture him on “respecting small tenants.” Meanwhile, Weed Wacker Ryan kept screaming every time he saw Roch, even though Roch was probably higher than all of them combined.

Soon enough Roch wasn’t just living under the couch—he was part of the chaos. Part of the smoke. Part of the Dudley Bud family. He joined every dumb idea, every late-night snack raid, every smoke session, chiming in with sarcastic comments and tiny puffs of roach smoke.

The Dudley Bud Unit had never been weirder… or funnier.
