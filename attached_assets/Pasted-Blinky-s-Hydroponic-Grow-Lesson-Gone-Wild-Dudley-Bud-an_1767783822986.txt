Blinky’s Hydroponic Grow Lesson Gone Wild
Dudley-Bud and Weed-Wacker Ryan visit Blinky’s shed for a simple hydroponic grow lesson, only to trigger “Power Bloom Mode” by touching every button they weren’t supposed to. Vines go wild, lights flicker like a rave, and Blinky nearly loses his sanity but the boys somehow create the fattest bush he’s ever seen. A fun, chaotic new chapter in the Dudley-Bud universe!

When science meets stoners, expect leaks, laughs, and leafy chaos.

Dudley-Bud had barely rolled out of bed when Weed-Wacker Ryan burst through the door, helmet still on, eyes wide like he’d just seen a discount sign at a bong shop. “Bro you won’t believe it. Blinky built a new hydroponic grow system in his shed… and he wants us to help.” Dudley-Bud blinked twice, rubbed his face, and muttered, “Hydroponics? Isn’t that just plants swimming?”

Five minutes later they were stumbling into Blinky’s shed, which looked less like a garden space and more like a mad scientist’s lab after a late-night YouTube binge. Tubes everywhere. Buckets humming. Lights glowing purple like a disco for lettuce. And in the middle stood Blinky, goggles fogged, hair statically charged to the ceiling. “WELCOME, apprentices,” he boomed, waving a turkey baster like it was a sacred staff.

Weed-Wacker Ryan nudged Dudley. “He’s in professor mode. Don’t say anything stupid.” Dudley nodded confidently, then immediately asked, “So… where do you put the soil?” Blinky’s soul visibly left his body for two seconds.

Lesson One: No soil. Only water and nutrients.
Lesson Two: Never EVER touch the red switch.
Lesson Three: Always respect the roots.

Within ten minutes they had already broken all three rules.

Ryan accidentally kicked a nutrient line, sending lime green plant juice across the shed like a sprinkler system with attitude. Dudley-Bud leaned on the control panel, flipping the forbidden red switch with the casual confidence of a man who does not understand consequences. And the roots? Well, Dudley stepped on them. Twice.

Suddenly the lights flickered, the water bubbled like a cauldron, and Blinky screamed, “OH NO YOU ACTIVATED POWER BLOOM MODE!” Every plant surged upward like they’d just been given a motivational speech and a Red Bull. Vines snaked around Blinky’s legs. Leaves slapped Ryan in the face. A tomato-sized bud ballooned right above Dudley’s head and exploded into a cloud of terpenes strong enough to make the shed soundproof.

Five minutes later, the chaos settled. The plants now the size of small trees swayed happily in their water buckets. Blinky collapsed on a milk crate, goggles crooked. Ryan and Dudley looked around like two toddlers who had accidentally built a spaceship.

Blinky sighed, half exhausted, half-proud.
“Well, boys… technically… you did complete the lesson.”
Dudley grinned. “So we pass?”
