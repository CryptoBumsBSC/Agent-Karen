DudleyBud and the Candy-Making Highjinks 🍬
DudleyBud and his crew set out to create the ultimate stoner candy — but chaos quickly followed. From gummy disasters to a chocolate fountain explosion and a raccoon stuck in cotton candy, their wild experiment turned into a sugary, psychedelic mess. In the end, they didn’t just make candy — they made memories (and maybe a new universe-bending fudge).

The ultimate stoner candy By DudleyBud
It started, as most Dudley420 tales do, with a puff of inspiration and a slightly melted gummy bear stuck to the couch.

DudleyBud had an idea:

“Let’s make candy so dank… it’s art.”

Enter Weed Wacker Ryan, who immediately showed up with a blowtorch, a mixing bowl, and zero actual knowledge of candy-making. Crunchwrap the raccoon came too, dragging a shopping basket full of mysterious sweet wrappers and one very stolen marshmallow gun.

They turned Dudley’s shack into a psychedelic candy lab.
Giant gummy nugs. Pot-infused lollipops shaped like Tasmanian devils. Sour Diesel belts that made your tongue tingle and your thoughts float.

“This one tastes like mango… but makes you forget what mango is,” said Ryan, already three licks into something glowing faintly purple.

Things went sideways fast.

Crunchwrap got stuck in the cotton candy spinner.

DudleyBud accidentally invented a taffy so sticky it pulled out two fillings and possibly a memory from 2009.

Ryan tried to vape a caramel.

And then... the Great Chocolate Fountain Incident of Doom™.

It overflowed, spilled onto the floor, and activated a long-forgotten stash buried under Dudley's floorboards. Suddenly, the room smelled like cocoa and cosmic dust.

A neighbor wandered in asking about the noise and walked out convinced he saw Willy Wonka riding a kangaroo.

They wrapped up the day covered in syrup, success, and some very deep thoughts about jellybeans having feelings.

Dudley lit a final joint, passed it to Crunchwrap (who declined because he was still spinning slightly), and said:

“Sweetness isn’t just in the sugar… it’s in the friends you make it with. And also in this fudge that may or may not bend time.” Awe Man
