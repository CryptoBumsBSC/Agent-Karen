HAPPY NEW YEAR 2026 — A DUDLEYVERSE MIDNIGHT
Midnight hits and the Dudleyverse explodes into colour, music, and pure stoner chaos. Dudley Bud leads the cheers, the crew brings the madness, and Nova slips in with a smooth strum that locks the vibe in place. Fireworks light the sky, laughter fills the air, and the whole community steps forward together—ready to build something legendary in 2026.

The Dudleyverse was already buzzing before midnight.

Fireworks were being prepped on rooftops, speakers rattled windows, and Dudley Bud stood at the centre of it all, laughing as the countdown boards flickered to life. The vibe was loose, smoky, electric. Weed-Wacker Ryan tuned the sound system past sensible limits. Gunja-Mae drifted through the crowd, calm as ever. Roach the Sh*t Talker was running his mouth like it was an Olympic sport.

Then Nova (#Nov) arrived.

Not loud.
Not announced.

Just a smooth presence sliding in from the edge of the party. Black coat catching the neon, joint resting easy, guitar hanging low. One slow strum cut through the noise  not killing the chaos, just guiding it. People didn’t even realise they’d gone quiet. Nova nodded once, let the chord hang, then blended back into the smoke like he’d always been part of the night.

That’s when Agent-Karen showed up.

Clipboard first. Voice already raised. Complaints locked and loaded.

She tried to shut it down  noise violations, permits, “this isn’t authorised.” Nobody listened. The crowd laughed. Fireworks test-popped early. Nova strummed again, louder this time, and the party surged right past her.

Karen’s face tightened.

She stepped away. Pulled out her phone.

Her voice dropped.

“Sir… yes. It’s happening again.”

A pause.

Her posture changed.

“…No, I understand. Yes. I’ll stand down.”

Another pause. Her jaw clenched.

“Yes, of course. I’ll handle it.”

She hung up.

Karen turned back to the party  quieter now, measured, suddenly unsure. No one knew who she’d spoken to. No name. No explanation. Just the sense that something higher up had weighed in.

From across the rooftop, Nova caught her eye.

He smirked.
Strummed once more.
Fireworks exploded overhead  green, purple, gold  shaking the sky as the clock struck midnight.

2026 arrived loud, unapologetic, and unstoppable.

Karen faded back into the crowd.
Nova vanished into the smoke.
The Dudleyverse roared forward together.

Somewhere else…
someone in charge smiled  calm, satisfied  knowing the balance had been restored.
